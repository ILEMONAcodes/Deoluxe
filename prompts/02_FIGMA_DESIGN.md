**Look and feel:** premium, confident, editorial. Use the attached UI reference for layout and interaction feel only (rounded 24px cards, pill category chips, floating pill bottom nav, large product imagery, pill buttons, stacked-card onboarding). Do NOT copy its content or brand.

Onboarding slides are mobile-only. See "Desktop layouts" in `SCREENS.md`.

**Also create:** a components page (buttons, chips, product card variants, size chips, quantity stepper, inputs, toasts, bottom sheet, nav) and a clickable prototype of Home → Product → Cart → Checkout → Success for **both mobile and desktop**.
